Our Campus Mission

About ReFind

"Find it again. Give it a new beginning."

The Campus Challenge

Every semester, thousands of graduating university students move out of dorms, leaving behind excellent textbooks, scientific calculators, study lamps, bicycles, and furniture. Meanwhile, incoming freshmen spend excessive amounts purchasing brand new supplies.

Standard online marketplaces charge high commission fees, involve complex courier shipping, and lack student verification. ReFind was created as an exclusive, localized university network where verified students connect directly for offline, in-person handovers.

Campus Verification

Only students with valid university email credentials can interact, trade, and message, establishing peer accountability.

Zero Waste Reuse

Giving usable goods a second life prevents campus waste, reduces student living costs, and fosters a circular economy.

Three Flexible Ways

Whether you want to sell at student-friendly prices, barter goods through exchanges, or donate for free to peers in need.

Architecture & Engineering

ReFind is built following strict software engineering principles: Django 5 server-rendered MVT architecture, transactional row-level concurrency protection (`select_for_update()`) to prevent double acceptance, PostgreSQL data persistence, and dynamic partial updates powered by HTMX and Tailwind CSS.
