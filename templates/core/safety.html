Handover Safety Guidelines

ReFind is an offline, in-person campus marketplace. Follow these practical recommendations for safe, smooth transactions with fellow students.

Meet in Public Campus Spaces

Always schedule handovers in well-lit, populated campus areas: university library lobby, campus cafeteria, student activity center, or departmental reception.

Inspect Before Handover

Take a minute to verify item condition, test electronic gadgets (turn laptops or calculators on), and ensure all accessories are present.

Communicate via ReFind

Keep conversations on the platform messenger so there is a documented conversation trail tied directly to the listing and user profile.

Report Suspicious Behavior

If a listing appears stolen, misleading, or inappropriate, use the "Report Listing" button to notify campus moderators immediately.
